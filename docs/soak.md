# Bot soak

`./game.sh soak [map] [minutes]` runs `tools/soak.tscn`: four of the game's course-riding bots (`game/g2g_bots.gd`) on one map, driven by dot-bot's `DotBotSoak` with dot-spawn's `DotSpawnWatch` for stuck and fell-out verdicts. The report is `.godot/test-logs/soak-<map>.json` and one `SOAK {...}` line. There is no combat here, so the kill check is off. There is no navigation mesh either, so off-nav and unreachable are off; a bot rides its stage or the route, and the course puts it back when it arrives, falls or runs out of `RIDE_SECONDS`.

First run, 2026-10-22, 0.5 simulated minutes per map, 4 bots, 62 of the 64 maps: 32 pass, 30 fail, all failures `stuck` (a bot within one radius for over 10 s). Tick cost is not the problem: p95 is 0 to 4 ms at 128 Hz, worst single tick 91 ms (surf_lt_omnific, map load or plan step), object and memory growth within limits everywhere.

- Only surf_beginner (pilot, 28 arrivals) and surf_intro (24) actually complete rides. Every other map is the rule-based route bot, and none of them arrived once in 30 s.
- Put back every tick (fixed 2026-10-22): the course floor was measured from the goals alone, and on a map whose finish is higher than the spawn the floor sat above the start, so the bot fell at tick 0. `G2GSurfCourse.settle_zones` now counts the start too. After the fix bhop_elate, exodus, fury_2, japan, pandora2_fix and kz_bhop_badg3s pass; bhop_arcane_v2, interloper, pit, tesquo_v2 and surf_omnibus are plain stuck (the route bot does not get anywhere in 30 s, dot-bot's). Were: bhop_arcane_v2, bhop_elate, bhop_exodus, bhop_fury_2, bhop_interloper, bhop_japan, bhop_pandora2_fix, bhop_pit, bhop_tesquo_v2, kz_bhop_badg3s, surf_omnibus.
- Stuck without being put back: bhop_aztec, bhop_badges2, bhop_crash_egypt, bhop_eazy, bhop_lego2, bhop_monster_jam, bhop_platformer_fxd, bunnyhop_pro, buses_from_hell_fixed, kz_bhop_lucid, kz_bhop_yonkoma, surf_animals, surf_aquaflow, surf_hologram, surf_interference, surf_lt_omnific, surf_mesa, surf_rookie, surf_vegetables.

## Stuck triage, 2026-10-22: every catalogue map passes

`./game.sh soak <map> 0.5 --trace` prints one `TRACE` line a second for the first bot (position, speed, grounded, route index, target), and `--why` logs at INFO (the "no stage for the bots" reason). After the triage all 59 maps with a zones file that load pass at 0.5 minutes; the 30 stuck ones were three different things.

- **Soak false positive: the plan wait.** The route plan is stepped by the game's own ticks (2 ms each), not by frames, so the soak's 40 frames never finished it and the bots were aimed straight at the finish through walls for the whole run. The soak now ticks until the plan is done (up to 60000 ticks, a few minutes of wall time on a big map) before it starts counting.
- **Soak false positive: no finish, no stage.** bhop_lego2, bunnyhop_pro, buses_from_hell_fixed, kz_bhop_lucid, kz_bhop_yonkoma and surf_animals have no END zone in their zones file, so `G2GSurfCourse` has no stage ("stage 0 has nowhere to arrive") and the bots stand at the spawn by design. They are reported with `has_goal` false, so the watch does not call them stuck. Giving those maps a finish zone is map work, not the bot's.
- **Real: wedged on a wall the route does not know.** bhop_arcane_v2, aztec, badges2, eazy, interloper, monster_jam, omnibus, tesquo_v2 and the surf maps had a bot hopping on the spot against a wall or in a pocket for the whole 30 s ride. The route bot now notices two seconds inside one metre and hops off the line for a second, a different side each time (`UNSTICK_TURNS`), and the bots layer puts a bot back after six seconds inside 1.5 m (`SETTLE_SECONDS`), under the watch's ten. Those maps still have no route file or a plan that reaches the finish: nothing arrives on them, the bots are only no longer parked.
- The no-finish maps are `NOT_COURSES` in `headless_imported.gd`, which the soak reads (`NO_FINISH`) and checks both ways: a listed map that gets a course, or an unlisted one without, fails the setup. Each listed map's zones file says why it has no finish. Only bhop_grove has a route file; the planner is `tools/route_plan.py` (see routes.md for why it stops short elsewhere).
