# Not yet

- **A Workshop map plays as a timer course only.** `provides.json` accepts subscribed maps of `map_kind` `surf`, `bhop`, `kz` and `race`; the solo map menu (M), the vote, `/mapmenu` and the HUD list them as `Name [Pack]` (`label()`), and an online session lists none. A chosen pack map loads through `DotMapSession` like any catalogue map: its `scene` is the world (it needs its own collision bodies), and its `zones` entry (a `DotTimerZoneSet` JSON in metres, the format `maps/*.zones.json` uses, with a `Kind.SPAWN` zone for where to stand) reaches the timer through `maps.zones_json`; with no `G2GMap` script the spawn is that zone's destination (`G2GGame._zone_spawn`), else (0, 2, 0). What a pack map does not get is everything a `G2GMap` or imported map adds: bonus courses and the survey, the surf bot's route plan and `bot_nav` (a bot quota on a pack map has no course; `G2GSurfCourse` asks the map for its spawn), the physics layout's `world` classification, and the lightmap, sky, props and brush-entity mechanics of an imported `.bsp`. A pack that wants those would ship a scene whose root script extends `G2GMap`, which `provides.json` code scanning marks `runs_code` and the site reviews; nothing here loads or tests that. Zones are the mapper's to write: a pack with no `zones` entry loads with no start, finish or pit. [testing](testing.md#the-suites)
